Model Gateway & Document Understanding Demo

IBM · Software Developer Intern · May – Aug 2026 · OpenShift · LLM serving · Web development

Overview

During my summer at IBM I worked on two projects: infrastructure that makes it easier for teams to use many AI models through one interface, and a feature for a document-understanding demo. Because this was internal work, I describe it at a high level here.

Model gateway

Teams wanted to experiment with many large language models, but each model had its own endpoint and setup. I built a lightweight gateway on an OpenShift cluster that routes requests to 10 different models through a single endpoint, so switching models is a one-line change instead of new integration work.

  • Routes between 10 hosted models behind one consistent interface.
  • Cut the time to onboard a new model by about 80%.

Key-value extraction for the Watson Document Understanding demo

I developed a key-value pair (KVP) extraction page for the Watson Document Understanding demo, which pulls structured fields (like names, dates and totals) out of documents.

  • Ships with 8 built-in schemas for common document types.
  • Lets users define custom schemas for the fields they care about.

What I took away

Building tools other engineers depend on: designing a simple interface that hides complexity, and making features that are easy to demo to people outside the team.
