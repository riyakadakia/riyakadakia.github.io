RaC: Recovery & Correction for Robot Learning

Robotic Caregiving and Human Interaction Lab · Mar 2025 – Aug 2025

Overview

Scaling human-in-the-loop recovery for long-horizon bimanual tasks.

What I did

  • Co-author on RaC: Robot Learning for Long-Horizon Tasks by Scaling Recovery and Correction (accepted, IEEE T-RO).
  • Built the VR teleoperation and multi-camera capture system for collecting bimanual, contact-rich, long-horizon demonstrations.
  • The human-in-the-loop recovery framework reached 78% success on real-world bimanual tasks with 10× data efficiency.
