VLM Articulation Perception & GR00T in Simulation

Momentum Robotics Lab · Feb 2026 – Present

Overview

Finding joints on mechanical objects and driving a VLA policy in Newton.

What I did

  • Perception pipeline that uses vision-language models to detect and localize articulation points, refined with Segment Anything pre-processing and grid-based coordinate refinement.
  • Integrated the GR00T N1.6 vision-language-action model into the Newton physics simulator, mapping camera input and language instructions to end-effector trajectories.
  • Ran manipulation experiments on xArm7 and bimanual Franka FR3 with inverse kinematics, gripper control and force-sensing feedback.
